Legal and product information

Privacy Policy

We respect user privacy and aim to collect and use the minimum information necessary to operate a privacy-preserving self-management app.

Information and permissions

CheckIn Duck uses Apple Screen Time APIs to help users manage daily app-based habits. The app only monitors usage for user-selected apps after the user grants explicit permission.

The monitored information is used solely to determine whether a configured daily usage threshold has been reached for a task.

What the app does not access

The app does not read content inside third-party apps. It does not collect messages, typed input, tap behavior, screen content, or other in-app interactions.

The app is not designed for hidden tracking, parental control, or surveillance. It is intended for personal self-management.

Data use

We do not sell personal data. If future versions add analytics, subscriptions, or additional service providers, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect those changes.

We encourage users to review this page periodically for the latest policy terms.

Data retention

We retain only the information reasonably necessary to operate the app, maintain user-requested support communication, and comply with legal obligations.

Usage status used for daily task completion is intended to remain limited to the app experience and is not used to profile behavior inside third-party apps.

Permission controls

Users can stop monitoring at any time by changing Screen Time or app authorization settings on their device, or by removing user-selected apps from a task inside the app.

If authorization is revoked, the app can no longer evaluate the relevant usage threshold for those selected apps.
